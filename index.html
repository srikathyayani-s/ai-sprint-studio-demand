AI Sprint Studio helps startups and mid-sized companies rapidly implement focused AI solutions without the cost, delay, or risk of hiring a full-time AI team. We deliver tightly scoped "AI Sprints" — short, outcome-driven engagements led by experienced senior engineers and AI specialists. Each sprint is designed to solve a specific business problem, automate a workflow, or prototype a high-value AI capability within 1–2 weeks.

This program is offered as part of the Skill Forward AI upskilling initiative — connecting companies with trained practitioners building in-demand AI skills through real-world project work.

The Process

How an AI Sprint Works.

Four focused steps from initial conversation to working solution in your hands.

1
Discovery Call

We identify the business problem, workflow, or opportunity area. A free 15-minute conversation to understand your goals, challenges, and where AI can make the most impact.

2
Sprint Definition and Pricing

We scope a focused AI sprint with clear deliverables and timelines. You'll know exactly what we're building, when it's due, and what success looks like — before we start. 40-hour sprint starting at $800, 80-hour sprint starting at $1,500.

3
Rapid Implementation

Our team builds and delivers the solution collaboratively with your stakeholders. You're involved throughout — not handed a black box at the end.

4
Handoff & Next Steps

You receive a working solution or prototype, recommendations, documentation, and optional follow-on support. Every sprint ends with something tangible in your hands.
